Religions Timeline

How the world's religions arose — in parallel, c. 2600 BCE – 2000 CE

Slide the timeline: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, double-click to zoom in, or jump with the era tabs and the overview window. Bars span each text's or event's scholarly date range — the shading is the uncertainty. Click any bar for details and sources. Dates marked approximate or disputed are honestly uncertain; nothing here is presented as settled that isn't.

Click a text on the timeline to see its description, ideas, and scholarly source.

Open source (MIT) — contribute on GitHub. v2 spans ten traditions from 2600 BCE to 2000 CE: Sumerian and Egyptian religion, Zoroastrianism, Judaism, the Vedic tradition and Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Chinese traditions, Christianity, and Islam.
